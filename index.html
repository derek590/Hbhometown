Our Story

A Marine's Mission —
Now Serving Home

From enlisted Marine to Major, from the battlefield to the boardroom — our founder has led in every environment imaginable. That experience doesn't retire. It transforms into something that serves the next generation.


H&B Hometown LLC was built on a simple belief: the same discipline, integrity, and servant leadership forged in the United States Marine Corps can build stronger businesses, stronger veterans, and stronger communities right here in southern Idaho.

📍 Proudly Rooted in Twin Falls, Idaho

What Drives Us

Three Pillars. One Purpose.

01

Veteran Support

Those who served deserve more than a thank-you. We provide transition coaching, employment readiness, peer mentorship, and advocacy — connecting veterans in our community to the resources and opportunities they've earned.

02

Leadership Development

Leadership isn't a title — it's a discipline. Drawing from Marine Corps principles and real-world executive experience, we train individuals and organizations to lead with clarity, accountability, and purpose.

03

Community Development

Strong communities don't happen by accident. We partner with local governments, schools, nonprofits, and businesses in southern Idaho to build the programs, connections, and infrastructure that make Twin Falls thrive.
